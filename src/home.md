## Who we are
We are a Discord community by gamers, for gamers, focusing on, but not limited to, Helldivers 2 and Minecraft.

## History
The Bomb Squad was founded by Doomstar on December 9th, 2024, because he quote "just wanted to". Almost immediately, Doom was joined by Matmux, who was appointed as The Bomb Squad's first captain (our title for admins). Three days later, our second captain, chaotic_berserker, joined. Captain Chaotic was later joined by A Very Sane Person and huggybear in the captain position. This quintet of admins stayed much the same until mid-2025. In June of that year, Captain Huggy was replaced by TankObliterator to bring a Minecraft network up alongside the Discord. That brings us to the present day.

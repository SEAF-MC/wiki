## Who we are
We are a discord community By gamers For gamers focusing on, but not limited to, Helldivers 2 and Minecraft.

## History
The Bomb Squad was founded by Doomstar on December 9th 2024 because he quote "just wanted to". Almost imediatly Doom was joined by Matmux who was appointed as The Bomb Squads first captian (our title for admins). Three days later our second captian, chaotic_berserker joined. Captain Chaotic was later followed buy A Very Sane Person and huggybear. This quintet of admins stayed much the same untill mid 2025. In June of that year Captain Huggy was replaced by TankObliterator to bring a minecraft network up along side the discord. That brings us to current day.
